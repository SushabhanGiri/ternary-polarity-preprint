## Scientific scope

The archived result is intentionally limited to a **conditionally viable numerical benchmark within a conventional product-discrete scalar effective field theory**. The record does not claim experimental confirmation, a new fundamental theory, broad novelty, unification, quantum gravity, generation counting, or a unique nonzero observable relation.
